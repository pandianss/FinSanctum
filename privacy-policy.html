1. Privacy Policy

FinSanctum is designed as a calm, local-first banking exam study companion for Indian banking professionals preparing for IIBF JAIIB and CAIIB examinations. We do not display advertisements, we do not integrate third-party ad tracking SDKs, and we never sell personal information.

A. Local On-Device Storage (Default Offline Mode)

  • FinSanctum works offline without requiring an account. Bookmarked formulas, saved/completed lessons, solved MCQ history, Habit Ring goals, 30-day study history, Daily Study Streak, custom exam dates, and Paper/Midnight theme preferences are stored locally on your device using Android Room SQLite and Jetpack DataStore.

B. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Backup (Firebase Auth & Cloud Firestore)

  • If you choose to enable cross-device sync via Sign in with Google (using Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication), FinSanctum stores your Firebase UID, optional Google display name, optional Google email address, and your study progress metadata inside a private per-user Cloud Firestore document.
  • Strict server-side Firestore security rules restrict read, write, and delete access exclusively to your authenticated UID. No other user can read, list, or modify your cloud backup.
  • All cloud data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS and at rest on Google Cloud infrastructure.

C. Cloud AI Features (Firebase AI Logic & App Check)

  • When you invoke the AI Concept Tutor, generate a fresh Formula Vault Numerical Case Study, or play a chapter in Studio AI Voice mode, only the relevant study question or chapter text is transmitted over HTTPS to Google Firebase AI Logic (Gemini models) to generate the response or audio narration.
  • Do not enter confidential bank customer data, account numbers, PAN, Aadhaar, or proprietary internal bank records into the AI Concept Tutor.
  • Device integrity attestation is handled via Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA Enterprise in release builds) solely for abuse and fraud prevention.

D. Google Play Billing

  • One-time Exam Passes are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. FinSanctum never receives or stores your UPI PIN, bank account details, or payment card numbers. Purchase entitlement metadata is queried from Google Play solely to unlock or restore active passes.

E. Children's Privacy

FinSanctum is intended for adult banking professionals (ages 18+) preparing for professional certification exams and is not directed to children under 13.

2. Account & Data Deletion

Instant Self-Service Deletion Inside FinSanctum (Recommended):
  1. Open the FinSanctum app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Account icon in the top bar (or open Account & Cloud Backup → Manage on the Today tab).
  3. Tap Delete Cloud Backup & Account and confirm.
  4. Optionally check "Also erase local study progress stored on this device" to wipe local DataStore records simultaneously.

What Is Deleted Immediately

  • Firestore Cloud Backup: Your private /users/{uid} document containing your display name, email, bookmarks, completed lessons, MCQ progress, streaks, and exam date settings is permanently deleted.
  • Firebase Authentication Record: Your Google sign-in account link in FinSanctum is permanently deleted.
  • Local Data (Optional or Offline Users): Both signed-in and offline users can tap Reset Local Study Progress inside Account & Backup or clear app storage via Android Settings → Apps → FinSanctum → Clear Storage.
  • Retention Note: Google Play order receipts remain governed by your Google Play account history (FinSanctum does not store payment credentials).

Email Deletion Request (If You Uninstalled the App)

If you have already uninstalled FinSanctum and would like us to verify or purge any remaining cloud backup tied to your Google email address, email sspandian.here@gmail.com from that email address with the subject "FinSanctum Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 7 days.
